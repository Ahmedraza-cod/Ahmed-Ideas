HTML Semantic & Non-Semantic Tags

List of commonly used HTML tags

Semantic Tags

Semantic tags clearly describe the meaning and purpose of the content.

  • <header>
  • <nav>
  • <main>
  • <section>
  • <article>
  • <aside>
  • <footer>
  • <figure>
  • <figcaption>
  • <details>
  • <summary>
  • <time>

Non-Semantic Tags

Non-semantic tags do not clearly describe the meaning of their content.

  • <div>
  • <span>

Note: <div> and <span> are the most common non-semantic elements.
