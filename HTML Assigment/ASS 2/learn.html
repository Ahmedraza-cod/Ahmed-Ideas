Fruits

Fruits are the edible parts of plants that develop from flowers and contain seeds. They are usually sweet, juicy, and rich in important nutrients like vitamins, minerals, and fiber. Fruits play a very important role in keeping our body healthy and strong. They help improve digestion, boost the immune system, and provide natural energy. Fruits come in many shapes, sizes, and colors, such as red, green, yellow, and orange. Common fruits include mango, apple, banana, watermelon, and orange. Some fruits grow on trees, while others grow on plants or vines. People eat fruits fresh, or use them in juices, desserts, and salads. Eating fruits daily is essential for a healthy lifestyle.




Mango

Mango is a tropical fruit that is known for its sweet taste and rich flavor. It has a soft, juicy pulp and a large seed inside. The outer skin can be green, yellow, or orange depending on ripeness. Mango is rich in vitamins A and C, which help improve eyesight and strengthen the immune system. It is widely eaten fresh or used in juices, desserts, and shakes.




Apple

Apple is a popular fruit that is round in shape and has a crisp texture. It comes in colors like red, green, and yellow. Apples are rich in fiber and antioxidants, which are helpful for digestion and heart health. Eating apples regularly can help maintain a healthy body and is often linked with the saying “An apple a day keeps the doctor away.”




Banana

Banana is a soft, sweet fruit with a thick yellow peel. It grows in bunches on plants. Bananas are an excellent source of potassium, which helps in maintaining proper heart and muscle function. They also provide instant energy, making them a great snack for students and athletes.




Water Melon

Watermelon is a large, round fruit with a green outer rind and juicy red flesh inside. It contains a very high amount of water, making it perfect for hydration, especially in hot weather. Watermelon also provides vitamins A and C and helps keep the body cool and refreshed.




Orange

Orange is a citrus fruit known for its tangy and slightly sweet taste. It has a thick, easy-to-peel skin and juicy segments inside. Oranges are very rich in vitamin C, which helps strengthen the immune system and protect the body from diseases. They are commonly eaten fresh or consumed as juice.
